SECTION 03 // SCAN CALIBRATION NOTES

Tips for Accurate Coke & Water Bottle Detection

  • 01. Frame the Full Silhouette: Keep the bottle cap, contoured waist or PET ribbing, and label centered inside the viewfinder reticle.
  • 02. Use Balanced Lighting: Clear plastic water bottles reflect bright glare; diffuse lighting helps reveal structural ribs and transparency.
  • 03. Read Confidence Tiers: Scores at 80% or above confirm a Verified Bottle Match; lower scores prompt adjusting distance or angle.
